# VetFlow — v2 backlog (deferred from v1)

Detailed spec for each deferred feature so it can be pulled into a plan-update once v1 is proven. Not scoped or scheduled — this is the parking lot.

## QR walk-in intake
Printed QR at the front desk. Owner scans → mobile form (owner name, pet name/species/breed, reason for visit, phone) → writes a walk-in record into the same owners/appointments tables. Dashboard gets a "walk-ins today" queue.
Pitch angle: kills the paper logbook; every scan captures a phone number now reachable by reminders.

## Post-visit review requests
Automated SMS/email a set number of hours after an appointment is marked complete: "Salamat for visiting [clinic]! How did we do?" linking to the clinic's Google review page. Optional rating gate — 4–5 stars route to Google, 1–3 route to a private feedback form so bad reviews get caught internally first.
Pitch angle: more Google reviews = more walk-in discovery; fully hands-off.

## Owner pet card / vaccine history
Tokenized link (no login), regenerated per pet: pet profile, full vaccine history with dates, what's due next, past visits — a mobile "pet passport." Feeds the v1 vaccine-due engine.
Pitch angle: owners stop losing paper vaccine cards; clinic looks modern.

## Extra channels — Viber & Messenger
Add Viber (huge in PH) and FB Messenger to the notify() module and the receptionist. Messenger closes the "plugs into your FB page" loop.
Why v2: needs per-clinic Meta/Viber business API onboarding — real friction, not core to the pitch.

## Payments / booking deposits
Optional refundable/creditable deposit at booking (GCash/Maya via PayMongo) to further cut no-shows.
Pitch angle: clinics with chronic no-shows will pay for this alone.

## Multi-clinic SaaS admin
Turn the single-clinic profile into a real tenant model: signup, per-clinic subdomain/slug, billing, admin console. The shift from "one deploy per client" to "clinics self-serve."
Why v2: only worth building once several paying clinics validate demand.

## Analytics deepening (stretch)
Beyond ₱-recovered: no-show rate over time, busiest slots/services, reminder→confirmation conversion, vaccine-campaign recovery rate. Turns the dashboard into a retention/"we make you money" tool.
